1 Overview & Commitment

Welcome to Nursing Officer BY MH, operated by PRIME MULTI SERVICES AND SUPPLIERS ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and rights of nursing professionals, students, and healthcare examination aspirants using our Android app and web portal (https://nursingofficer.web.app).

This Privacy Policy clearly outlines what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is secured, and your full rights to manage or permanently delete your account and information.

2 Data We Collect

We only collect minimal, purpose-specific data required to run test series, progress tracking, and secure account management:

  • User Account Information: Your name, email address, optional contact number, and district when registering via Google Sign-In or email.
  • Academic Test Performance: Practice quiz answers, mock exam submissions, test completion time, bookmarks, and scorecards used to calculate percentiles and All-India Rank (AIR).
  • Device Diagnostics: Basic non-identifying telemetry (operating system version, device model, crash logs) to maintain high performance and fix bugs across phone models.

3 How Your Data is Used

  • To generate real-time exam results, percentile calculations, and personalized mistake notebook analysis.
  • To securely sync your mock test progress across your mobile phone and browser.
  • To send timely alerts regarding official nursing examination dates (NORCET, ESIC, DHS/DMER) and important syllabus updates.
  • To safeguard account security and prevent unauthorized access or system misuse.

4 Third-Party Cloud Infrastructure

Our platform uses trusted, enterprise-grade cloud providers for data hosting and authentication:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase: Provides encrypted database storage, secure authentication, and cloud messaging. Google conforms to ISO 27001, SOC 2, and GDPR standards.
  • Google Play Services: Manages genuine application updates and crash-free runtime on Android devices.
Strict Non-Disclosure Guarantee: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information, mobile numbers, or test records with third-party advertisers, marketing agencies, or data brokers.

5 Data Retention & Permanent Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may request permanent deletion of your account and all associated test scores at any time:

  • Direct Email Request: Send an email from your registered address to gitevijay123@gmail.com with subject "Delete My Nursing Officer BY MH Account".
  • Processing Time: All personal credentials, quiz attempts, and profile records are completely purged from our database within 7 to 15 business days.
